# Product Strategy

## Positioning

PulseShield should feel like a private COVID and respiratory health radar for everyday life. The opportunity is to make illness guidance useful all year: respiratory illness, crowded places, school outbreaks, travel, clinics, and vulnerable-family visits.

The product should stay original. It should not copy any government COVID app, contact-tracing workflow, repository structure, wording, or visual pattern. Its own wedge is personal plan advice, private place memory, illness focus modes, and transparent local scoring.

The sharper promise: PulseShield helps you choose the safest version of the plan you already had.

## Core loop

1. User opens the app before a risky activity.
2. User picks a focus: COVID-like, flu/cold, respiratory, or vulnerable visit.
3. App combines local symptoms, exposure, check-ins, and area signals.
4. User gets a plain decision: go, adjust, limit, or stay home.
5. User can still inspect the score, reasons, and three next actions.
6. Check-ins stay local unless the user chooses a privacy-preserving community notice.
7. The app earns retention by being calm, fast, and useful without surveillance.

## Product wedge

- Students: campus venue risk, class check-ins, and outbreak-aware study spaces.
- Families: safer visits to elderly or vulnerable relatives.
- Venues: privacy-preserving place memory and quieter-time guidance.
- Local communities: trusted advisories without identity tracking.
- Employers: optional workplace health safety dashboards with no individual tracking.

## MVP

- Daily check screen.
- Illness focus selector with focus-specific scoring and advice.
- Private place memory.
- Local-only check-in history.
- Risk engine with transparent rules.
- Decision layer above the risk score.
- Trust dashboard.
- Official guidance links by country or region.

## Success signals

- Users can understand why a recommendation changed in under 10 seconds.
- A safer alternative is visible before the user abandons a plan.
- Check-ins and symptoms are useful without requiring an account.
- High-risk states route people toward trusted guidance before optional venue features.
